### AI & I by Every — How Anthropic Uses Claude Fable 5 With Mike Krieger

**The Takeaway:** Claude Fable 5 has crossed a threshold where the model is no longer a tool you prompt, but a teammate you delegate to — and that fundamentally changes how you think about software engineering, productivity, and what it means to build.

Anthropic Labs head and Instagram co-founder Mike Krieger joins Dan Shipper to share what it's actually like to use Claude Fable 5 day in and day out — not just the first-day wow factor, but the real workflow shifts that happen after weeks of use.

The biggest change? Krieger says he felt "like a total newbie again" because his prompting style was suddenly outdated. Fable 5 doesn't just execute tasks; it understands intent, plans architecture, and even pushes back on code review feedback. He describes setting up complex tasks before bed and waking up to find the model not only finished the work but also handled unexpected failures (like a remote service going down) by scaffolding a temporary backend and documenting what it did.

"I think the most impressive thing for me is being able to delegate that kind of level of task and just trust that the right thing will happen by the end," Krieger says. "It really feels like now it's a teammate that I can delegate a lot of work to."

Krieger demonstrates a personal media tracker app he built over a weekend — but the real innovation is that the app is self-modifying. A long-press on a chat button lets you request a feature change, and Claude edits the app live, previews it via Vercel, and shows a diff view. "Does this thing need to be a production-level thing that's gonna go to a million users? No. But it felt really good to have something where I felt like it didn't have to stop at just the weekend."

On the question of whether software engineering is over, Krieger is nuanced: "Software engineering is different. It is dramatically changed." The craft of writing code line by line, dreaming about elegant solutions — that part has passed. But the broader practice of software production — understanding user needs, verifying outputs, handling incidents in production — is "alive and well and essential." He emphasizes that ownership and accountability still matter: "You ultimately, as a person, still need to stand behind the work that you are doing."

Krieger also touches on the cost question. Fable 5 is expensive, but he argues the real metric isn't per-turn cost — it's cost to complete a task to satisfaction. "Fable 5 has really shined for me, which is it actually just does it right so that I don't have to go spend nine or ten subsequent turns."

**核心要点：** Claude Fable 5 已经跨越了一个门槛——它不再是一个你输入提示的工具，而是一个你可以委派任务的队友。这从根本上改变了你对软件工程、生产力以及"构建"本身的理解。

Anthropic Labs 负责人、Instagram 联合创始人 Mike Krieger 与 Dan Shipper 分享了日复一日使用 Claude Fable 5 的真实体验——不是第一天的惊艳感，而是数周使用后真正发生的工作流转变。

最大的变化是什么？Krieger 说他感觉"自己又成了一个完全的新手"，因为他原来的提示方式突然过时了。Fable 5 不仅仅是执行任务；它能理解意图、规划架构，甚至在代码审查反馈中提出异议。他描述说，睡前设置好复杂任务，醒来发现模型不仅完成了工作，还处理了意外故障（比如某个远程服务宕机），方法是临时搭建一个后端并记录下所做的一切。

"对我来说，最令人印象深刻的是能够委派那种级别的任务，并相信最终会得到正确的结果，"Krieger 说。"现在感觉它真的像一个我可以委派大量工作的队友。"

Krieger 展示了他花一个周末构建的个人媒体追踪应用——但真正的创新在于，这个应用是可以自我修改的。长按一个聊天按钮，你可以请求修改某个功能，然后 Claude 会实时编辑应用，通过 Vercel 预览，并显示差异视图。"这个东西需要成为一个面向百万用户的生产级产品吗？不需要。但能拥有一个感觉上不必止步于周末项目的东西，感觉真的很好。"

关于软件工程是否已经终结的问题，Krieger 的回答很有分寸："软件工程已经不同了。它发生了巨大的变化。"一行一行写代码、梦中都在思考优雅解决方案的手艺——这部分已经过去了。但更广泛的软件生产实践——理解用户需求、验证输出、处理生产环境中的事故——"依然活跃、健康且至关重要"。他强调，所有权和责任感仍然重要："作为一个人，你最终仍然需要为你所做的工作负责。"

Krieger 还谈到了成本问题。Fable 5 很贵，但他认为真正的衡量标准不是每次交互的成本，而是将任务完成到满意程度的成本。"Fable 5 真正让我惊艳的地方在于，它实际上一次就把事情做对了，这样我就不必再花九到十轮去反复修改。"
