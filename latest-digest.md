### Training Data — Google DeepMind's Logan Kilpatrick: Why the Model Eats the Harness

**The Takeaway:** The distinction between "the model" and "the scaffolding around it" is collapsing — and the model will eat the harness. The real alpha for startups isn't in building your own agent harness, but in vertical domain expertise that big labs can't afford to focus on.

Logan Kilpatrick runs Google AI Studio and the Gemini API at Google DeepMind. In this wide-ranging conversation, he covers everything from Google's agentic strategy to why coding agents feel like narrow superintelligence, and how Google thinks about the tension between maximizing user outcomes vs. maximizing eyeballs.

**On the model eating the harness:** "The models are now being spun up in containers and sort of have an agent harness and all that stuff. The scaffolding is oftentimes a couple of steps ahead of what is baked directly into the model, and then what ends up happening is the model eats that scaffolding and it becomes part of the native model system." Logan predicts that within 12 months, the agent harness as we know it will largely be absorbed into the model itself. "The alpha will be somewhere else now. It won't be in trying to spin your own harness because the model just does it natively."

**On where startups can win:** "If you have expertise in that domain, you know the customers, you know the ecosystem, you can really run laps around even the best model labs because focus is the superpower of startups." Google, by contrast, has "an obligation to do a bunch of things as a big company." He sees more opportunity for startups now than 24 months ago, not less — especially with coding agents closing the gap on established codebases.

**On narrow superintelligence:** Logan believes we've already reached narrow superintelligence in coding. "Coding is just so good that it does kind of feel like narrow superintelligence." But he emphasizes that this hasn't taken away from human developers — it's an accelerant. "I feel like I have more agency in the world. I feel like I can tackle more ambitious problems."

**On Google's agentic strategy:** Google is in "crawl" mode for most products, with the Gemini app and Antigravity closest to "walk." The agent harness (Antigravity) is becoming the new through line across Google's products — replacing the Gemini API as the connective tissue. "Coding has proved to be the general purpose agent harness in addition to also working really well for coding."

**On Omni and world models:** Omni is a true single model (not routing between models) that blurs the line between world models and video models. Logan's favorite use case: editing video backgrounds without changing the person. "It's not changing me. It is changing a bunch of these other bits, which are not me."

**Memorable quote:** "We can't let other people make the world a better place more than we can. We're all fighting over who can make the world better more than the other person, which when you frame it like that, seems really goofy."

**核心观点：** "模型"和"模型周围的脚手架"之间的界限正在消失——模型将吞噬 harness。初创公司的真正 alpha 不在于构建自己的 agent harness，而在于大型实验室无法聚焦的垂直领域专长。

Logan Kilpatrick 负责 Google AI Studio 和 Gemini API。在这场内容丰富的对话中，他涵盖了从 Google 的 agentic 战略到为什么 coding agents 感觉像是 narrow superintelligence，以及 Google 如何思考最大化用户成果与最大化眼球时间之间的张力。

**关于模型吞噬 harness：** "模型现在在容器中启动，内置了 agent harness 等等。脚手架往往比直接嵌入模型的东西领先几步，然后发生的事情是模型吞噬了这些脚手架，成为原生模型系统的一部分。" Logan 预测在 12 个月内，我们今天所知的 agent harness 将 largely 被吸收到模型本身中。"alpha 会在别处。不会在于自己搭建 harness，因为模型原生就能做到。"

**关于初创公司的机会：** "如果你在那个领域有专长，你了解客户，了解生态系统，你甚至能跑赢最好的模型实验室，因为专注是初创公司的超能力。"相比之下，Google "作为大公司有义务做很多事情。"他认为现在初创公司的机会比 24 个月前更多，特别是 coding agents 缩小了与成熟代码库的差距。

**关于 narrow superintelligence：** Logan 认为我们在编程领域已经达到了 narrow superintelligence。"编程已经好到感觉像是 narrow superintelligence。"但他强调这并没有削弱人类开发者——它是加速器。"我感觉自己在世界上有更多的能动性。我能解决更雄心勃勃的问题。"

**关于 Google 的 agentic 战略：** Google 大部分产品处于"爬行"阶段，Gemini app 和 Antigravity 最接近"行走"。Agent harness (Antigravity) 正在成为 Google 产品的新贯穿线——取代 Gemini API 成为连接组织。"编程已经被证明是通用 agent harness，同时它也非常擅长编程本身。"

**关于 Omni 和世界模型：** Omni 是一个真正的单一模型（不是模型间的路由），模糊了世界模型和视频模型之间的界限。Logan 最喜欢的用例：编辑视频背景而不改变人物。"它没有改变我。它改变的是那些不是'我'的其他部分。"
