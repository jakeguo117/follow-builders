**The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck — Stripe's AI Chief: How AI Agents Will Buy, Sell, and Pay**

**The Takeaway:** The infrastructure for agentic commerce is already deployed and real — agents are buying, selling, and paying today — but the truly transformative shift will come when agents become full-fledged economic actors running businesses end-to-end, not just assisting humans with purchases.

Stripe's Head of Data and AI Emily Sands returned to The MAD Podcast to update Matt Turck on where agentic commerce stands one year after their first conversation. The picture is more concrete than you might think.

Sands laid out a spectrum of agentic commerce, borrowing the self-driving car framework (Level 1-5 autonomy). Most consumer interactions today hover around Level 2 — the AI helps find products, but humans still make the final decision. Level 3, where you give an agent a budget and say "get it done," is still rare. But on the business-to-business side, fully autonomous agent-to-agent transactions are already happening through Stripe's Machine Payments Protocol (MPP).

The infrastructure Stripe has built is worth unpacking. The **Agentic Commerce Protocol (ACP)** — built with OpenAI — lets businesses expose their product catalog once and opt into all agents that support the protocol. Think of it as MCP for commerce. Brands like Best Buy, Coach, and Kate Spade are already on it, as are platforms like Shopify and Wix. The **Shared Payment Token** is a new primitive that lets agents pay without ever seeing the underlying credentials — the token encodes exactly what the agent is allowed to do (which merchants, up to what amount, for how long). The **Link Wallet for Agents** gives consumers guardrails: set budgets, approve individual transactions, or let the agent spend within limits.

One of the most under-discussed topics in AI, Sands argued, is **token theft**. "Fraudsters have figured out that in AI, you actually don't really need to steal money or credentials. You can just steal tokens." More than one in six signups at AI companies are multi-account abuse — fraudsters hammering signup flows to get free credits. Free trial abuse has more than doubled on Stripe in the last six months, mostly driven by AI companies. And because tokens have real marginal cost (unlike SaaS), this fraud is existential for margins.

The most provocative idea Sands floated: **agents as micro-firms**. "It's not Emily permissioning an agent to buy on her behalf. It's Emily has an agent who's tasked with running a business, and that includes buying some things and selling some things and making some profits." She expects to see concrete examples of this within 12 months.

Sands also touched on **vibe deployment** — the neglected sequel to vibe coding. "The barrier to building is gone, but the barrier to deploying is a real friction." Stripe's CLI now sees 70% of its API resource requests coming from agents, not humans. Stripe Projects was built to let agents sign up for, configure, and integrate all the services needed to deploy an app — directly from the command line.

On the macro side: new business registrations are surging globally (up 40% in the Netherlands, 70% in Finland, 80% in France). Stripe Atlas startups from the 2026 cohort are tracking to **5x the revenue** of last year's class at the same point. And 5 million Americans now make their living as solopreneurs earning over $100K/year.

**The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck — Stripe 的 AI 负责人：AI Agent 将如何购买、销售和支付**

**核心观点：** Agent 商业的基础设施已经部署并真实运转——agent 现在就在购买、销售和支付——但真正的变革性转变将发生在 agent 成为端到端运营业务的完整经济主体，而不仅仅是协助人类购买的时候。

Stripe 的数据与 AI 负责人 Emily Sands 再次做客 The MAD Podcast，向 Matt Turck 更新了距离他们第一次对话一年后，agent 商业的现状。画面比你想象的更加具体。

Sands 借用自动驾驶框架（L1-L5 自主级别）描绘了 agent 商业的光谱。目前大多数消费者交互停留在 L2 左右——AI 帮助发现产品，但人类做最终决定。L3，即给 agent 一个预算说"搞定它"，仍然罕见。但在 B2B 端，完全自主的 agent 对 agent 交易已经在通过 Stripe 的 Machine Payments Protocol (MPP) 发生。

Stripe 搭建的基础设施值得拆解。**Agentic Commerce Protocol (ACP)**——与 OpenAI 共同构建——让企业只需一次性地公开产品目录，然后选择加入所有支持该协议的 agent。可以把它理解为 MCP 的商用版。Best Buy、Coach、Kate Spade 等品牌已经接入，Shopify、Wix 等平台也已采用。**Shared Payment Token** 是一种新的支付原语，让 agent 无需看到底层凭证即可支付——token 精确编码了 agent 被允许做什么（哪些商家、最多多少钱、持续多久）。**Link Wallet for Agents** 则为消费者提供了护栏：设置预算、审批单笔交易，或让 agent 在限额内自由消费。

Sands 认为，AI 领域最被低估的话题之一是 **token 盗窃**。"欺诈者已经发现，在 AI 领域，你其实不需要偷钱或凭证。你只需要偷 token。"超过六分之一的 AI 公司注册是多账户滥用——欺诈者反复冲击注册流程以获取免费积分。过去六个月，Stripe 上的免费试用滥用翻了一倍多，主要来自 AI 公司。由于 token 有真实的边际成本（不像 SaaS），这种欺诈对利润率来说是生存级的。

Sands 提出的最 provocative 的想法是：**agent 作为微型企业**。"不是 Emily 授权一个 agent 替她购买。而是 Emily 有一个负责运营业务的 agent，这包括购买一些东西、销售一些东西、创造一些利润。"她预计 12 个月内就会出现具体的例子。

Sands 还谈到了 **vibe deployment**——vibe coding 被忽视的续集。"构建的障碍已经消失，但部署的障碍是真正的摩擦。"Stripe 的 CLI 现在 70% 的 API 资源请求来自 agent，而不是人类。Stripe Projects 的诞生就是为了让 agent 能够直接从命令行注册、配置和集成部署应用所需的所有服务。

宏观层面：全球新企业注册量激增（荷兰增长 40%，芬兰 70%，法国 80%）。2026 届 Stripe Atlas 初创公司的收入追踪数据是去年同期的 **5 倍**。美国现在有 500 万人作为年收入超过 10 万美元的独立创业者谋生。
