### No Priors — How Nuclear Will Unlock Energy Abundance with Valar Atomics Founder Isaiah Taylor

**The Takeaway:** The nuclear industry has been a modeling and simulation industry for decades — but the real unlock is hardware iteration, and Valar Atomics just proved it by turning on the first advanced reactor ever built by a startup.

**The Episode:**

Isaiah Taylor, founder and CEO of Valar Atomics, sat down with No Priors at their Utah nuclear facility — standing in front of the first advanced reactor ever to make power by a startup, and the fifth new nuclear device to make power in the United States since 2000. His background is unusual for nuclear: no credentialed decades of research. Instead, he grew up thinking about nuclear (his great-grandfather was a Manhattan Project physicist), watched the industry fail to iterate for years, and eventually started Valar out of sheer frustration that no one was solving the problem at the pace required.

Taylor's core insight is that nuclear has never had its Ford or Toyota moment — it's never been built for mass scale. Most nuclear companies are actually modeling and simulation companies that produce beautiful "paper reactors" but never turn anything on. Valar's different philosophy: this is a hardware execution problem, not a design problem. "We would rather have a simpler machine that's easier to build at higher scale than a machine that's more efficient."

The company measures itself by "tick rate" — the time between each new reactor going critical. From founding to first atom split: 2 years, 4 months. From first to second reactor: 7 months. The goal is to get that down to minutes. Taylor is explicit about the strategy: build on equity balance sheet rather than waiting for project finance, verticalize anything that prevents scale (from inventing their own concrete to building their own reactor protection system for $400K instead of the quoted $5M), and focus ruthlessly on simplicity and safety.

The safety philosophy is worth highlighting. Traditional nuclear reduces risk by reducing *odds* of failure — elaborate cooling systems, redundancies, endless engineering. Valar reduces risk by reducing *consequence* — their reactor is designed so that even if everything fails, it can't melt down. They're about to demonstrate this by scramming the reactor and then turning off *all* electrical systems, relying on passive circulation to cool the core.

Taylor's vision goes beyond powering data centers. "Energy is the fundamental input to the quality of human life," he says. With AI and robotics converting human labor into energy consumption, he argues that "energy will become the cost of all things" — and making energy 10x cheaper means making everything effectively free.

**Memorable quote:** "We are willing to verticalize anything that is necessary on our path to scale, and we've proven that a couple times in this reactor so far in areas that we were told repeatedly was utterly impossible."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5Xvbq_zvOQ4

**核心观点：** 核能行业几十年来一直是一个建模和仿真行业——但真正的突破口是硬件迭代，而 Valar Atomics 刚刚证明了这一点，他们启动了有史以来第一个由初创公司建造的先进反应堆。

**内容概要：**

Valar Atomics 创始人兼 CEO Isaiah Taylor 在 No Priors 的 Utah 核设施现场接受了采访——他们身后就是第一个由初创公司发电的先进反应堆，也是 2000 年以来美国第五个新的核能发电装置。他的背景在核能领域很不寻常：没有几十年的核研究资历。他从小就在思考核能（他的曾祖父是曼哈顿计划的物理学家），多年来目睹行业无法迭代，最终因"没有人以所需的速度解决问题"而感到极度沮丧，于是创办了 Valar。

Taylor 的核心洞察是：核能从未经历过它的福特或丰田时刻——从未为大规模量产而设计。大多数核能公司实际上是建模和仿真公司，生产精美的"纸上反应堆"，但从未真正启动过任何东西。Valar 的哲学截然不同：这是一个硬件执行问题，而不是设计问题。"我们宁愿要一个更简单、更容易建造、可以大规模量产的机器，也不要一个效率更高但更复杂的机器。"

公司用"tick rate"来衡量自己——每次新反应堆达到临界状态之间的时间间隔。从公司成立到第一次分裂原子：2 年 4 个月。从第一个反应堆到第二个：7 个月。目标是把这个时间缩短到几分钟。Taylor 的策略很明确：用股权融资而非项目融资来建设；垂直整合任何阻碍规模化的事物（从发明自己的混凝土到以 40 万美元自建报价 500 万美元的反应堆保护系统）；对简洁性和安全性保持极致专注。

安全理念值得特别关注。传统核能通过降低故障的*概率*来降低风险——复杂的冷却系统、冗余设计、无尽的工程。Valar 则通过降低*后果*来降低风险——他们的反应堆设计使得即使所有系统都失效，也不会发生堆芯熔毁。他们即将通过一次演示来证明这一点：紧急停堆后，关闭*所有*电力系统，仅依靠被动循环来冷却堆芯。

Taylor 的愿景远不止为数据中心供电。"能源是人类生活质量的根本投入，"他说。随着 AI 和机器人将人类劳动转化为能源消耗，他认为"能源将成为所有事物的成本"——让能源便宜 10 倍，就意味着让所有东西都变得几乎免费。

**金句：** "我们愿意垂直整合任何在规模化道路上必要的东西，而在这个反应堆中，我们已经多次在那些被反复告知'绝对不可能'的领域证明了这一点。"
