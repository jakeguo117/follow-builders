**The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck — State of Enterprise AI 2026: Aaron Levie on Tokenmaxxing, Rise of Headless, and AI-Proofing Your Job**

**The Takeaway:** The biggest bottleneck in enterprise AI isn't the technology — it's that model breakthroughs keep happening faster than companies can implement any standard architecture, paradoxically slowing down real-world deployment.

**The Takeaway:** 企业 AI 最大的瓶颈不是技术——而是模型突破的速度超过了企业实施任何标准架构的速度，这反而拖慢了实际部署。

Box CEO Aaron Levie sits down with FirstMark VC Matt Turck for a wide-ranging conversation on the state of enterprise AI in 2026. Levie has a uniquely privileged vantage point: he runs a public company selling to the world's largest enterprises (GE, Procter & Gamble, Morgan Stanley) while being deeply embedded in Silicon Valley's agentic AI scene. This gives him a clear-eyed view of the gap between what's possible and what's actually deployable.

Box 的 CEO Aaron Levie 与 FirstMark 的 VC Matt Turck 进行了一场涵盖 2026 年企业 AI 现状的广泛对话。Levie 拥有独特优势视角：他经营着一家向全球最大企业（GE、宝洁、摩根士丹利）销售产品的上市公司，同时又深度嵌入硅谷的智能体 AI 场景。这让他对"可能实现什么"和"实际能部署什么"之间的差距有着清醒的认识。

The most striking insight? The pace of AI progress is itself a deployment problem. "The breakthroughs keep happening faster than the customer can implement any kind of standard architecture," Levie says. "And those breakthroughs oftentimes make obsolete the last thing you implemented. It's this really bittersweet thing — the technology is getting so advanced that it makes obsolete the prior thing that you implemented, which actually means that the rollout takes longer."

最引人注目的洞察是什么？AI 进步的速度本身就是部署问题。"突破的速度超过了客户实施任何标准架构的速度，"Levie 说。"而这些突破往往让上一次实施的东西过时。这是个苦乐参半的事情——技术变得如此先进，以至于让之前实施的东西过时，这实际上意味着推广需要更长的时间。"

On token costs — arguably the hottest topic in enterprise AI right now — Levie is characteristically nuanced. He rejects the simple narrative that labs were "subsidizing" costs, instead pointing out that agents can now consume $1,000 of compute on a single task, making the old $20/user/month subscription model impossible. The real shift? AI costs are escaping the IT budget (3-7% of revenue) and moving into line-of-business OpEx budgets. "This is actually good for the AI industry because you're no longer constrained by IT spend budgets," he says. "But now the CMO has to decide: do I spend a million dollars on compute or a million dollars on marketing events?"

关于 token 成本——这可以说是当前企业 AI 中最热门的话题——Levie 给出了富有深度的见解。他拒绝了"实验室在补贴成本"的简单叙事，而是指出智能体现在可以在单个任务上消耗 1000 美元的计算资源，这使得旧的 20 美元/用户/月的订阅模式不可行。真正的转变是什么？AI 成本正在逃离 IT 预算（占营收的 3-7%），进入业务部门的 OpEx 预算。"这实际上对 AI 行业是好事，因为你不再受 IT 预算的限制，"他说。"但现在 CMO 必须决定：是把 100 万美元花在计算上，还是花在营销活动上？"

Levie is surprisingly bullish on jobs. His argument: most companies weren't saturated with talent before AI — they were constrained by it. When a Caterpillar or Eli Lilly engineer gets 5x productivity, that company will take on 5x more projects and need more engineers, not fewer. "The doomers are also wrong about jobs," he says flatly. "This is going to be a very real sustaining job. It's not a one-time thing where you implement the agent and it works forever."

Levie 对就业出人意料地乐观。他的论点是：大多数公司在 AI 之前并不是人才饱和——而是受到人才限制。当一个 Caterpillar 或 Eli Lilly 的工程师获得 5 倍生产力时，这家公司会承担 5 倍的项目，需要更多而不是更少的工程师。"末日论者对就业的看法也是错的，"他直截了当地说。"这将是一个真实存在的持续性工作。这不是一次性的事情——你部署了智能体，它就永远运行下去了。"

He also coins a useful distinction between AI coding and AI for the rest of knowledge work. Coding has a highly technical user, models hyper-trained on code, verifiable outputs (code either runs or it doesn't), and a codebase that contains all the context. Knowledge work has none of these advantages — context lives across 20 different systems, access controls are a mess, and the "last mile" of legal, financial, or medical work requires human judgment. "I can't have an agent adjust the liability rate in a contract and then just ship it," he notes.

他还提出了一个有用的区分：AI 编程与 AI 用于其他知识工作。编程有高度技术性的用户、在代码上超训练的模型、可验证的输出（代码要么能运行要么不能），以及包含所有上下文的代码库。知识工作没有这些优势——上下文分布在 20 个不同的系统中，访问控制一团糟，法律、金融或医疗工作的"最后一英里"需要人类判断。"我不能让智能体调整合同中的责任率然后就直接发货，"他指出。

For employees worried about their jobs, Levie's advice is practical: spend 5-10% of your time getting fluent with these tools. Use Codex, use Cowork, use Perplexity Computer. "Stop your cable subscription, spend that $50 a month on agents, and just start using them. Figure out what they can do. Then ask yourself: what would I do if I had an unlimited chief of staff?"

对于担心自己工作的员工，Levie 的建议很务实：花 5-10% 的时间熟悉这些工具。用 Codex，用 Cowork，用 Perplexity Computer。"取消你的有线电视订阅，把那 50 美元花在智能体上，然后开始使用它们。弄清楚它们能做什么。然后问问自己：如果我有一个无限的参谋长，我会做什么？"
